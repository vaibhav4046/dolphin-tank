## Not demonstrated by me (stated, not hidden)
- Precedence between errors where the spec is silent (401 vs 400 missing key; 403 vs 409 vs 404 beyond the cases above): not asserted.
- Seeded `captured_amount` defaults, seeded holds up to 2^53, the same payment listed in two authorizations: spec-silent author choices, observed only (captured seed reads captured_amount = amount, payment_id null, payment_ids []).
- Chromium only; no screen reader. Contrast measured on text nodes against the effective background colour (no gradient text on these screens).
- The upgrade browser check uses a small same-origin proxy (stage-1 has no UI): API calls go to the stage-1 build until the import and to stage 2 afterwards; the page, assets and HTML always come from stage 2.

## Infrastructure noise (kept, not hidden)
The shared WSL VM restarted and stepped its wall clock a few times during the session (docker containers exited 255, `ConnectionRefused` in `s2_race` run 2 and `s2_export` run 5, one 20 s worker timeout in `s2_race` run 3, and three timing assertions of mine in `s2_export`/`s2_export_expiry` that slept by a computed delay instead of waiting on the wall clock; their logs show `clock sanity` failing in the TEST before any product assertion). Every one of those was rerun clean after the test was fixed to wait on the wall clock; the failing logs are kept under `logs/` with a descriptive suffix. One further unexplained transient: `b1_quality` run 1 hung 30 s on `/` after logout (not reproduced in 3 full reruns and a 20-iteration stress, `browser/probe3.py`).
