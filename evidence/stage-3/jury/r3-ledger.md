## Honest notes on my own runs

- r3_sweep seed 4, first run: 4 FAIL lines on one assertion ("created_at lies within its own request window +-30 ms"): three consecutive stamps 60 ms ahead of the request window. Cause: this WSL2 VM steps its wall clock back ~0.8-1.0 s about every 32 s (`logs/wsl_clock_steps.log`: -820, -1037, -983 ms in 120 s); a strictly monotonic stamp source then leads the wall clock until it catches up. Not a service defect. I added `checks/clk.py` (a monitor thread widens the window by the steps it saw) and reran all new scripts; the log of that first run was replaced, the failing line is kept in `logs/wsl_clock_steps.log`.
- r3_seedmatrix, first run: 16 FAIL "import #2 ... unchanged": my test bug (re-import of the ORIGINAL export compared with a snapshot taken after an extra payment). Fixed; rerun 1407/0.
- r3_legacy_import, first trial: one self-check failed (history had 7 authorizations, threshold 10); I enlarged the history; rerun 33/0 on three seeds.
- t3_statement seed 21: 71 pass / 1 fail in both the first pass (3c7c411) and now: my generator drew no effective-time tie ("at least one tie exercised"); every oracle assertion passes. Seeds 7 and 45 pass completely.
- run_regression.sh: three docker host-port collisions on random ports (they hit c03, c07 and c09 which ran without their second container and passed; c08, which needs it, passed 74/0).
