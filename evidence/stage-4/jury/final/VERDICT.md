## Observations (not rejections)
- Wallet route `/` renders four `h2` and no `h1` (every other route has an `h1`); `/login` and `/signup` "Log in" link measures 40 x 44 px. Both match what the operator disclosed in FACTORY.md; neither is a stated requirement (24 px WCAG 2.2 AA target minimum is met; no B-row asks for an `h1`).
- After a refund whose response was lost but which committed, a later feed refresh (for example after another refund) shows "Nothing left to refund on this payment." beneath the still-displayed "Unconfirmed ... retry sends the same refund once" notice for that payment; no retry button remains. The page does learn the committed state and money moved once, so the outcome is correct; the stale notice text is cosmetic.
- Four refusal texts (403 forbidden, 404 not_found, 422 invalid_refund_target, 422 validation_failed) cannot be produced by the UI against the real service (it never offers the control to a non-receiver or on a refund payment); they were checked with mocked envelopes via `route.fulfill` and the mapping to plain text passes. Real-service refusals checked: refund_exceeds_payment, insufficient_funds.
- No batch-correction screen exists in the browser; RUN.md states they are an operator-only API, and the specification asks for no screen.
