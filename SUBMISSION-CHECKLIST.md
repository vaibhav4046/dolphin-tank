Source: official WeAreDevelopers x BAND participant guide, inspected 6 October 2026. This records limits, not eligibility certification.

- [x] Public repo, README, FACTORY, full room export, stage folders and matching mandates.
- [x] At least three seats and reciprocal two-seat exchange: offline check passed.
- [x] Generic mandate check passed.
- [ ] Fresh isolated all-stage run at final product revision; independently verified clean-container gate 3.
- [ ] Final video shows BAND room, actual seat handoff and resulting app.
- [ ] Final presentation PDF/video file accepted by submission form.
- [ ] Final receipt retained.

Autonomy requires dispatches to be the only human input. Three extra provider-limit resumes remain disclosed. Do not claim full autonomy or promise withheld-test results.
